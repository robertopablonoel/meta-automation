## KEY CHALLENGES & PAIN POINTS

### Pain Point 1: Desire That Won't Turn On
She isn't broken and she isn't uninterested in her partner — the *wanting*
just doesn't arrive the way it used to. She can decide to have sex, but she
can't decide to crave it. The absence of that spark scares her more than the
absence of the sex itself.

### Pain Point 2: The "Get In the Mood" Tax
To feel anything, she feels she needs a runway — wine, the perfect night, zero
stress, kids asleep, nothing on her mind. Real life almost never delivers that,
so intimacy keeps getting postponed. She resents needing a substance or a
perfect setup to access what used to be automatic.

### Pain Point 3: Touch Has Become a Question
A back rub isn't a back rub — it's the opening of a conversation she doesn't
want to have. So she creates small distances: goes to bed first, stays busy.
Not because she doesn't love him. Because she doesn't want to disappoint him
*again*.

### Pain Point 4: Skeptical From Being Burned
She's a researcher. She's bought the pretty-bottle "libido" supplements that did
absolutely nothing. Every dud reinforced "maybe this is just who I am now." She
now assumes most of what she sees on TikTok is a scam — and she's half-hoping
to be proven wrong.
